Why OutKin exists

Family is also something we find.

OutKin is an LGBTQ+-rooted, friendship-first community for adults who want relationships with more depth than a feed, a swipe, or a follower count can offer. It is built around the idea that some of the people who become family are the people we find along the way.

Find your people

Traditional pen pals, supportive friendships, cultural exchange, everyday companionship, and relationships that may naturally grow into chosen family.

Letters, not feeds

The experience is built around one-to-one correspondence and thoughtful profiles rather than public popularity mechanics.

Privacy by design

Email addresses and mailing addresses are not public profile fields. Physical address sharing uses a separate mutual-consent process between established pen pals.

Platonic by design

OutKin is not a dating service. Chosen family means adult platonic friendship that becomes meaningful through trust and time—not assigned parent/child roles.

What chosen family does—and does not—mean here

Members can say they value consistency, encouragement, cards, traditions, milestones, and long-term supportive friendship without having to disclose trauma or explain why those things matter. OutKin does not match people for money, housing, therapy, guardianship, employment, or emergency support.

Public early access

The community is still growing.

OutKin is open to the public while the experience continues to improve. Early members may encounter a smaller match pool and occasional rough edges. Member feedback directly helps shape what comes next.
